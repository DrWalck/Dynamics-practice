Key equations: rigid-body kinematics

Fixed-axis rotation

Gears in mesh: r_Aω_A = r_Bω_B (no slip).

Relative velocity

r_B/A points from A to B. For CW rotation, ω = −ω k̂.

Instantaneous center

Where lines perpendicular to two known velocity directions cross. v = ω r_IC for any point. A rolling wheel's IC is its contact point. Use it for velocities only.

Relative acceleration

Solve all velocities first; the ω² terms need them.
