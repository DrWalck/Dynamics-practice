Key equations: rigid-body kinetics

Newton–Euler

Fixed pivot O: ΣM_O = I_Oα. Accelerating point P: ΣM_P = I_Gα + (r_G/P × ma_G).

Kinematics you'll need

Solve velocities first (instantaneous centers help), then accelerations.

Moments of inertia

Parallel axis: I = I_G + md².

Work & energy

T₁ + ΣU₁→₂ = T₂. Normal forces at smooth pins and rolling contacts do no work.

Impulse & momentum

Conservation of angular momentum

With no external moment about a point (like the system's center of mass in space), I₁ω₁ = I₂ω₂ about that point.
