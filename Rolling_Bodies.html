Key equations: rolling bodies

Rolling without slipping

Friction is static and can be anything up to μ_s N. Check it at the end.

Equations

Moments about the contact point C are allowed for rolling without slipping on a flat or straight surface.

Energy

Static friction at the contact does no work while rolling without slipping.

Rolling with slipping

Friction is kinetic, f = μ_k N, opposing sliding. v_G and ωr are independent until they become equal.

Shape factor I_G/(mr²)

Hoop 1, shell ⅔, cylinder ½, sphere ⅖. Smaller factor rolls faster.
