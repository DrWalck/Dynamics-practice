Key equations: impacts

Conservation of momentum

Holds when the only large forces during the impact are between the two bodies.

Coefficient of restitution

Velocity components along the line of impact. e = 1: elastic. e = 0: plastic (they stick).

Oblique impact (smooth)

Along n: momentum + e. Along t: each body keeps its own velocity component.

Rigid bodies with a pin

The pin exerts an impulse, so linear momentum is not conserved; angular momentum about the pin is. Apply e to the velocities of the contact points.

Energy

Kinetic energy is lost unless e = 1. Never use work–energy across an impact; use it before or after.
