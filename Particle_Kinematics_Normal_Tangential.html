Key equations: normal & tangential coordinates

When to use N–T

The path is known (a circle, a curve, a track). Polar coordinates are position-based geometry; N–T is path-based geometry.

Velocity

Velocity is only tangential. v = ds/dt is the speed along the path.

Acceleration
