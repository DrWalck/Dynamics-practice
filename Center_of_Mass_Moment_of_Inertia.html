Key ideas: choosing the right moment of inertia

What I measures

How hard it is to change the angular velocity of this mass distribution about this axis. Always name the axis.

1. Particle or rigid body?

A particle has I_G = 0 but still has angular momentum about another point:

A rigid body has I_G and rotational KE ½I_Gω².

2. About its own center or another point?

Only between G and a parallel axis. I_G is the smallest.

3. Is the mass location fixed or changing?

Fixed: ΣM_O = I_Oα. Sliding mass (like a collar): I changes, so use ΣM_O = dH_O/dt:

Center of mass & composite bodies

Holes count as negative mass. Radius of gyration: I = mk².

Common shapes (about G)

Slender rod about its end: ⅓mL².
