Key equations: kinetics in normal–tangential coordinates

Equations of motion

ê_n always points toward the center of curvature.

When to use n–t

The path is known (a circle, a curve, a loop). Constant speed still means a_n = v²/ρ ≠ 0.

Energy + Newton

Two positions: get v from work–energy first (forces normal to the path, like tension or N, do no work), then use ΣF_n = mv²/ρ for the force.

Tangential with a chain rule

Use v dv/ds when the distance along the path is known.

Banked curve

Upper signs: about to slide up (v_max). Lower signs: about to slide down (v_min).
