Key equations: polar coordinates & relative motion

When to use polar

Motion is described from a fixed point O: a rotating arm, a collar on a rod, a cam, a spiral. The frame is still inertial; only the basis vectors ê_r and ê_θ rotate.

Velocity

v_r = ṙ (radial), v_θ = rθ̇ = rω (transverse).

Acceleration

−rθ̇² is centripetal; 2ṙθ̇ is the Coriolis term. It is a real kinematic acceleration here, not a fictitious force.

Caution

ṙ and r̈ are not the speed and a_r is not dv/dt. a_r = r̈ − rθ̇² includes the centripetal part.
